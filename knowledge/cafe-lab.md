# The bigger plan: the café-lab

## The café-lab in one line

> A small team of three engineers that runs playful technology experiments in the real city, films how people react, and grows that audience into a physical café-lab where people come to drink coffee and play with the experiments themselves.

## Roadmap

_Six months of audience, then the café_

| Phase | When | Focus | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Launch | Months 0–2 | Starter maker kit, build the tag platform, first 5 experiments, start posting | A video travels + posting rhythm holds |
| 2 · Grow | Months 2–4 | AI-powered experiments, weekly series, Secret Sign season 1, 3 free brand pilots | Steady audience + café money secured |
| 3 · Prepare | Months 4–6 | Find the location, start café permits, budget and funding, one pop-up test | Pop-up works + permits in hand |
| 4 · Open | Month 6+ | Café-lab opens, the Sign reveals the address, workshops + lab | — |

The bottle wall fits Phase 2 as the channel's first big “hero” season, with its Ramadan finale landing just before the café opening.

## Channel identity

Science channels run experiments with chemistry and physics; this one runs them with technology, people and the city. Every video follows six beats:

question → hypothesis → build → test → result → lab note

Experiments are numbered (EXP-001…), the look is a lab notebook, and failures get posted too. Derja on camera, French and English subtitles.

## The first five episodes

1. Does AI speak Derja? — the three founders test AIs on Derja (no budget, no location).
2. Us vs. AI — who writes the better Derja song hook? Strangers vote blind.
3. AI invents a drink, baristas make it — three cafés, one AI recipe.
4. Which café is the most curious? — song stones with NFC tags in five cafés.
5. The barista compliment chain — one kind note travels across five cafés.

## Brand experiments: free first, paid later

Three free experiments with real brands, posted as content, then the 4th brand pays. Cap free pilots at three; each must earn permission to post, a testimonial, a budget contact, and real numbers.

Slots: 1. Free pilot, 2. Free pilot, 3. Free pilot, 4. Paid. Each free pilot must earn: permission to post, a testimonial, a budget contact, real numbers.

## Rules for every experiment

- Ask before posting anyone's face; children only with a parent's OK.
- Owner's permission before placing or filming anything.
- No personal data — anonymous counts only.
- Everything people send is moderated.
- Link to music, never host it.
- Prizes must be real.
